How it works

1. Landmarks

Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker finds 478 points on your face, locally via WebAssembly.

2. Geometry

Symmetry, canthal tilt, eye spacing, facial thirds, jaw/cheek ratio, face proportions, mouth/nose ratio.

3. Your score

Everything runs in your browser. Your scores are saved only on this device, and you can delete them anytime.

Please read: this is an entertainment tool that measures photo geometry, not your worth or real-world attractiveness. The ideals are rough heuristics, not science. Lighting, lens distortion, head angle and expression shift the numbers, and attractiveness depends on far more than proportions (skin, expression, style, personality). If rating apps affect how you feel about yourself, it's fine to skip them.
